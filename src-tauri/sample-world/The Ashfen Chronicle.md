# The Ashfen Chronicle

A rough history of the Ashfen Vale, pieced together from Thornhaven's records and the accounts of [[Mira]] — who has lived near these marshes long enough to remember things she should not.

```timeline
# The Order Takes the Keep
Date: Year 0

The [[The Order of Embers]] finishes construction of [[Locations/The Ember Keep.md]] and establishes its arcane library in the lower halls. The Order's roads are laid across the marsh.

# [[Lore/The War of Embers.md]] Begins
Date: Year 7

Three city-states form a coalition against the Order. They argue the library is a threat. They are not entirely wrong. The siege of [[Locations/The Ember Keep.md]] begins.

# The Library Is Sealed
Date: Year 12

The Order seals the library behind a puzzle lock before the final breach. Survivors scatter into the [[Lore/The Ashfen Marshes.md]]. The coalition appoints no one to hold the ruins.

# Thornhaven Endures
Date: Year 312 — Present

[[Locations/Thornhaven Village.md]] survives as a market village at the marsh's edge. [[Characters/Mira Ashvale.md]] camps near the keep and watches for movement no one else has noticed.
```
