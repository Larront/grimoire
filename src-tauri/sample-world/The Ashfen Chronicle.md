# The Ashfen Chronicle

The history of the Ashfen Vale, as far as anyone can piece it together from Thornhaven's tax rolls, a handful of coalition dispatches and the recollections of [[Mira]], who remembers things about this marsh that she should not.

```timeline
# The Keep Is Raised
Date: Year 0

[[The Order of Embers]] completes [[Locations/The Ember Keep.md]] and moves its library into the lower halls. Raised stone roads go down across the marsh so that scholars can reach the keep dry-shod.

# [[Lore/The War of Embers.md]] Begins
Date: Year 5

Three city-states, allied for the first and last time, declare the library a danger to them all. Their armies lay siege to [[Locations/The Ember Keep.md]].

# The Library Is Sealed
Date: Year 12

With the walls failing, the Order seals its library behind a puzzle lock. The upper towers fall days later. The Order's survivors vanish into the marsh.

# Thornhaven Endures
Date: Year 312 — Present

[[Locations/Thornhaven Village.md]] has outlasted every other village on the marsh's edge by trading in what the marsh grows. [[Characters/Mira Ashvale.md]] has camped in sight of the keep for three years, watching for movement no one else has noticed.
```
