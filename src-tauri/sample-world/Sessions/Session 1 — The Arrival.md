# Session 1 — The Arrival

**Date:** Night of the autumn equinox  
**Location:** [[Locations/Thornhaven Village.md]]  
**Present:** Four players

## Summary

The party reached Thornhaven at dusk, soaked through after three days on the marsh roads. They took rooms at the Muddy Boot and found [[Mira]] at the bar, arguing with Osric over the price of goat-weed.

## Events

1. [[Mira]] warned the party off the road to [[Locations/The Ember Keep.md]]. It is closed, she said, in the sense that anyone who takes it after dark comes back changed or does not come back. Pressed for more, she described the markings on the library door, as her family had handed them down, and nothing else.
2. [[Characters/Aldric Vane.md]] watched the party from a corner table, and left without a word when one of the players met his eye.
3. One player searched the abandoned temple and came away with a carved stone fragment she cannot read. [[Characters/Mira Ashvale.md]] saw it later that evening and said nothing, though she plainly knew what it was.

## NPCs Met

- [[Characters/Mira Ashvale.md]] — sold the party three herb packets for a silver they could barely spare
- [[Characters/Aldric Vane.md]] — watched; did not approach

## Loot and Rewards

- Three herb packets: one for marsh fever, one for marsh itch, and one whose label does not say
- A carved stone fragment from the temple (a piece of the puzzle lock, not yet identified)

## Notes

The carved fragment matches the description [[Mira]] gave of the puzzle-lock markers. She has not said so out loud. She is waiting to see what the party does with it first.

> [!note] What she recognised, and did not say
> The script on the fragment is her own family's. If a player offers it to her unprompted, she will trade the first turn of the puzzle lock for it. If they hide it, she will be on the eastern road ahead of them.
