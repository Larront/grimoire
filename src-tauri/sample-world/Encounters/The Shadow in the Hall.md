# The Shadow in the Hall

A hunt in the lower halls of [[Locations/The Ember Keep.md]]. Something has lived down here since [[Lore/The War of Embers.md]], three centuries in the dark, and it does not want company.

## Description

Somewhere past the first stair, the party realises they are not alone. The thing is fastest where the dark is complete, and it knows these halls better than any map of them. It has been waiting a long time for someone to come down.

Once an archivist of [[The Order of Embers]], twisted over three hundred years as the library's containment seals slowly failed. It cannot speak, but it knows [[Mira]]'s family name, and it hesitates when it hears it.

## Loot

- A fragment of stone tablet carrying part of the puzzle lock's notation. Meaningless without the other pieces.
- A sealed vial labelled in the Order's script. [[Characters/Aldric Vane.md]] recognises it on sight and will not say how.

Run this after the party has met [[Mira]] and [[Characters/Aldric Vane.md]], so the Lurker's hesitation means something when it comes.
