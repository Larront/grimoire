# Welcome to the Ashfen Vale

*A fog-wrapped corner of the world — yours to wander before you build your own.*

The Ashfen Vale is a marshland on the border of a fallen empire. A ruined fortress overlooks it from the east. A wandering herbalist knows every secret path through it. Beneath the keep's foundations, a library sealed three centuries ago still waits.

This sample world shows what a populated Grimoire ledger looks like. Explore it before building your own.

## Where to begin

- [[Locations/The Ember Keep.md]] — a crumbling fortress, a sealed library, and something that dislikes the light.
- [[Characters/Mira Ashvale.md]] — the person who knows everything the keep wants kept secret.
- [[The Ashfen Chronicle.md]] — a lore note with an embedded Timeline tracing three centuries of history.
- [[Sessions/Session 1 — The Arrival.md]] — a session log from the first night the players arrived in Thornhaven.

## What to notice

- **Sidebar folders** — notes are organised into Characters, Locations, Encounters, Sessions, and Lore.
- **Backlinks panel** — open any note and see which other notes link to it.
- **Graph** — see how all notes, maps, and stubs connect. Look for [[The Order of Embers]] — a stub note: referenced but not yet written.
- **Map** — the Ashfen Region map has pins linked back to location notes.
- **Alias links** — "Mira" resolves to [[Characters/Mira Ashvale.md]] because she has aliases set in her note. [[Mira]] is the same note.
- **Scenes** — layered audio embedded right in the page. Scroll down to *Set the scene* and press play.
- **Infobox** — the summary panel a wiki page keeps in its corner. [[Locations/Thornhaven Village.md]] has one; [[Characters/Mira Ashvale.md]] has one with a portrait.
- **Callout** — a boxed aside. [[Locations/The Ember Keep.md]] holds text to read aloud when the party reaches the gate.
- **Image** — [[Locations/The Ember Keep.md]] again: its picture is pinned to the right of the Description, with the prose running past it.
- **Statblock** — a creature you play on rather than read. Open [[Encounters/The Shadow in the Hall.md]] and take a few hit points off the Lurker; the note on disk changes with it.

> [!tip] The panel, the box and the picture are all blocks
> Everything the four bullets above point at was inserted with `/` on an empty line, and every one of them is still plain markdown in the file. This aside is a Callout, added the same way.

## Set the scene

Scenes layer a music bed under an ambience loop and play them together. Press play — then expand the mixer to balance each layer.

```scene
# Boss Battle
Id: 1
```

```scene
# Town Market
Id: 2
```

You can drop a scene into any note this way, or manage them all from the **Scenes** tab.

When you are ready to make this world yours, choose **Make this world mine** from the sidebar.
