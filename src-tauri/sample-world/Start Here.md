# Welcome to the Ashfen Vale

*A fog-bound corner of somebody else's world. Wander it first, then build your own.*

The Ashfen Vale is marsh country on the edge of a fallen empire. A ruined keep watches over it from the east, and beneath the keep lies a library that has been sealed for three hundred years. One herbalist knows every path across the marsh. She is not saying which of them lead to the library door.

Everything here is a note, a map or a scene, linked together the way a GM's own prep would be. Open things, follow the links, change whatever you like.

## Where to begin

- [[Locations/The Ember Keep.md]] — the ruin, the sealed library, and whatever lives in the dark beneath them.
- [[Characters/Mira Ashvale.md]] — the herbalist who knows more about the keep than she lets on.
- [[The Ashfen Chronicle.md]] — three centuries of history, laid out on a Timeline.
- [[Sessions/Session 1 — The Arrival.md]] — the players' first night in Thornhaven, kept as a session log.

## What to notice

- **Folders** — the sidebar sorts the world into Characters, Locations, Encounters, Lore and Sessions. Your own world can use whatever folders suit it.
- **Backlinks** — open any note to see every other note that links to it.
- **Graph** — every note and every link at once. Look for [[The Order of Embers]]: a stub, which is a note the world mentions but nobody has written yet.
- **Map** — the Ashfen Region map has three pins (the keep, Thornhaven and Mira's camp), and clicking one opens its note.
- **Aliases** — Mira's note lists "Mira" as an alias, so [[Mira]] finds her just as her full name does.
- **Scenes** — music and ambience layered together and played from the page. There are two below, under *Set the scene*.
- **Infobox** — the at-a-glance panel a wiki keeps in its corner. [[Locations/Thornhaven Village.md]] has one, and [[Characters/Mira Ashvale.md]] has one with a portrait.
- **Callout** — a boxed aside. [[Locations/The Ember Keep.md]] uses one for the text to read aloud when the party reaches the gate.
- **Image** — also in [[Locations/The Ember Keep.md]]: a picture set to the right of the description, with the text running alongside it.
- **Statblock** — a creature you run rather than read. In [[Encounters/The Shadow in the Hall.md]], take a few hit points off the Lurker and watch the note itself change.

> [!tip] The last four are all blocks
> The panel, the box, the picture and the statblock were each added by typing `/` on an empty line, and each is still plain markdown in the file. This aside is a Callout, added the same way.

## Set the scene

A scene lays a music bed under an ambience loop and plays the two together. Press play, then open the mixer to balance the layers.

```scene
# Boss Battle
Id: 1
```

```scene
# Town Market
Id: 2
```

You can drop a scene into any note this way, or find them all under the **Scenes** tab.

When you want to keep this world, edits and all, choose **Make this world mine** in the sidebar.
