A weathered herbalist who trades in remedies and, more quietly, in information. She has walked every safe path through [[Lore/The Ashfen Marshes.md]] and trusts no one who has not bled for what they know.

## Description

Mid-forties, with grey-streaked hair tied back and a satchel heavy with dried plants and stoppered vials. She talks in short sentences and always knows where the door is.

## Personality

Practical above all. She will help anyone who is already helping themselves, and she never warns the same person twice.

## Background

Born in [[Locations/Thornhaven Village.md]], she has spent twenty years out on the marsh. One of her ancestors was inside [[Locations/The Ember Keep.md]] when the library was sealed, and she claims to know part of the puzzle lock's answer. Whether she does is another matter.

## Notes

Her camp sits within sight of the keep's western approach, and she has not moved it in three years. She and [[Characters/Aldric Vane.md]] keep an uneasy truce, built on what each knows about the other.
