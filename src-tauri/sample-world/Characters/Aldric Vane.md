# Aldric Vane

A former soldier who lives on the edge of [[Locations/Thornhaven Village.md]]. Four years ago he went with the expedition that tried to open the sealed library, and he was one of the few who came back. He does not talk about what they found.

## Description

Late fifties, with a scar along his jaw and a long knife he never sets down. He starts drinking before noon and never seems to get drunk.

## Personality

Stubborn and haunted. He respects plain speaking and has no patience for anyone who asks about the keep. He will lie to protect something; he will not lie to gain anything.

## Background

His family fought for the coalition in [[Lore/The War of Embers.md]], and the story handed down with the name was that something in the library belonged to them by right. He came to Thornhaven to claim it. Whatever he saw inside [[Locations/The Ember Keep.md]] changed his mind about wanting it.

## Notes

He and [[Mira]] keep an uneasy truce. She knows what he found down there. He knows she knows. Neither has said it aloud.
