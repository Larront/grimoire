A ruined fortress on the eastern edge of [[Lore/The Ashfen Marshes.md]], built by [[The Order of Embers]] and held by it until [[Lore/The War of Embers.md]].

## Description

![The outer wall from the causeway, warnings and all](.grimoire/images/the-ember-keep.png){align=right width=40%}

The upper towers came down in the siege. The lower halls survived, and something that hates the light has made them its own. The outer walls are cut with warnings in a script no one in Thornhaven can read. Whether they were meant to keep people out or to keep something in, nobody has settled.

> [!read-aloud] The party reaches the gate
> The causeway ends where the gate used to be. Two broken towers lean into each other above you, and the marsh has come up through the flagstones to meet them. The carvings on the wall are cut so deep that three centuries of rain have not softened them. Whoever made them meant them to be read from here: from this side, at this distance, by someone standing exactly where you are standing.
>
> Nothing moves. The lower halls are somewhere below, and the light stops at the doorway.

## Notable Features

- **The Puzzle Lock** — on the door of the underground library. Unopened in three centuries. [[Mira]] claims to know part of the answer.
- **The Carved Warnings** — cut into the outer walls, in the same script as the panels in Thornhaven's old temple.
- **The Western Approach** — the footing is treacherous, [[Mira]] says, and the warnings on this side are not for decoration.

## NPCs

[[Mira]] camps within sight of the western approach: close enough to watch, far enough to run. [[Characters/Aldric Vane.md]] went inside four years ago and will not discuss what he found.

## Notes

No one from [[Locations/Thornhaven Village.md]] will spend a night inside. Ask the regulars at the Muddy Boot why, and they look at you as if you had asked why fire burns.
